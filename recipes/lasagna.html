Lasagna

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions; drain.
  3. In a skillet, brown ground beef over medium heat; drain excess fat.
  4. In a bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, salt, and pepper.
  5. Spread a layer of marinara sauce in the bottom of a baking dish.
  6. Layer 3 noodles, half the ricotta mixture, half the mozzarella, and a third of the marinara sauce.
  7. Repeat layers, ending with noodles and remaining sauce on top.
  8. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese.
  9. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  10. Remove foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes, or until cheese is bubbly.
  11. Let cool for 10 minutes before slicing.
