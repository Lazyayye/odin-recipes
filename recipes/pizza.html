Pizza Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 packet active dry yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Your favorite toppings (pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers, etc.)

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, yeast, sugar, and salt.
  2. In a separate bowl, mix the warm water and olive oil.
  3. Gradually add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients, stirring until a dough forms.
  4. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5 minutes, until smooth.
  5. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let it rise for 1 hour.
  6. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
  7. Roll out the dough on a floured surface and transfer it to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  8. Spread the pizza sauce over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust.
  9. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese on top, then add your favorite toppings.
  10. Bake for 12-15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  11. Slice and enjoy your homemade pizza!
