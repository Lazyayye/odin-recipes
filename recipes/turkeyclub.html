Turkey Club Sandwich Recipe

Ingredients

  • 3 slices of bread (white or whole wheat)
  • 4 slices of cooked turkey breast
  • 2 slices of cooked bacon
  • 2 leaves of lettuce
  • 2 slices of tomato
  • Mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Toast the bread slices until golden brown.
  2. Spread mayonnaise on one side of each slice of bread.
  3. On the first slice, layer 2 slices of turkey, 1 slice of bacon, and a leaf of lettuce.
  4. Add the second slice of bread on top, mayo side down.
  5. On the second slice, layer the remaining turkey slices, the second slice of bacon, tomato slices, and the second leaf of lettuce.
  6. Add the final slice of bread on top, mayo side down.
  7. Secure the sandwich with toothpicks if necessary, and cut it diagonally into two halves.
  8. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  9. Serve immediately and enjoy your delicious Turkey Club Sandwich!
